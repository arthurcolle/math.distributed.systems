# Finite-temperature tropicalization error

For a nonempty finite family \(a_1,\ldots,a_n\in\mathbb R\), \(n\ge1\),
and \(\beta>0\), set \(m=\max_i a_i\). Then

\[
e^{\beta m}\leq\sum_i e^{\beta a_i}\leq n e^{\beta m}.
\]

Monotonicity of the logarithm and division by positive \(\beta\) give

\[
m\leq \beta^{-1}\log\sum_i e^{\beta a_i}
\leq m+\beta^{-1}\log n.
\]

This is classical. Its role in the atlas is to fix the normalization and
exhibit the nonuniformity when the state count grows with \(\beta\). It is
not a proof of a polymer-to-KPZ or large-deviation bridge.
